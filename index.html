How PackPal works 〰

Three simple steps, designed for staff who have their hands full.

1📅

Take bookings

See remaining places for each day. The alpha checks capacity and vaccination status before accepting a booking.

2🐾

Care for dogs

Check vaccination warnings and add one-tap notes for ate, walked, played and napped.

3💌

Keep owners happy

Preview one dog's daily notes in Pupdate. Secure owner login and live sharing are planned, not yet built.
